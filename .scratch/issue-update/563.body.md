## Version and provenance contract
All proposals carry server-owned `schema_version`, `contract_version`, `prompt_version`, `model_id`, `model_version`, `builder_version`, `tuning_version`, `input_snapshot_hash`, and `provenance_id`. A proposal is stale/invalid when any required version differs from the active contract or when the input snapshot hash no longer matches; the server never silently migrates proposals across versions.

Provenance is bounded to `proposal_id`, `source_role`, `requesting_scope`, `input_snapshot_hash`, schema/contract/prompt/model versions, `canonical_event_sequence`, `outcome`, `reason_code`, `normalized_output_hash`, timestamps, and `correlation_id`. Raw prompts, credentials, private context, and unrestricted generated text are not persisted in provenance. Normalized bounded proposal content is retained separately only after validation and under storage policy.
